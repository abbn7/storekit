# Luxury design audit findings

The current Hero image and editorial typography are strong and should remain unchanged. The solid Navbar and simplified icon treatment read cleaner than the former glass-heavy version. Collections captions now use a restrained border-top and no black glass panel, which fixes the previously reported visual problem.

The remaining visible issue on desktop is the Featured Collections composition: the first card spans two rows while the other two cards are short landscape cards, leaving a large unused white area below the right-hand cards. This reads as unfinished rather than intentional luxury. The section should use three equal editorial cards on desktop, with a consistent portrait ratio and the same caption treatment.

The current product and section surfaces are also moving in the right direction after removing glow, pills, and heavy glass. Further changes should stay restrained: solid backgrounds, thin rules, compact square controls, warm ivory/ink/gold palette, and generous whitespace.

After changing Collections to three equal 4:5 cards, the desktop composition is balanced: the former large empty area is gone, all three images share the same visual weight, and the captions remain readable over a restrained gradient with a thin rule. This is the preferred direction for the luxury storefront.
